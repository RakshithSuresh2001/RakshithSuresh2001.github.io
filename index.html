Signed off · tapeout at IHP handled by Symbiotic EDA

RISC-V SoC with an 8x8 int8 systolic array

PicoRV32 (RV32IM), two 4 KB SRAM macros, an SPI boot loader and UART, and a 16-pad IO ring in a QFN32 package. It also carries a 2 KB SRAM block for silicon validation, on its own supply and detachable by a strap pin. Signed off with KLayout only, and built and checked automatically on Symbiotic EDA’s self-hosted BlueGarden CI/CD.

Gate-level simulation of a power-on self-test hung on the first multiply. I traced it to X-pessimism in a counter with no reset, proved the logic correct with a probe, and documented it as a simulation-only waiver.
